US 12,507,882 B2

      
Adaptive and Radially
Expanding Speculum

      
Inventor: Arlette Geller  ·  Assignee: RL3T LLC  ·  Dec. 30, 2025

    


    

      
Abstract

      
An adaptive and radially expanding speculum is provided. The speculum includes an expansor configured for being inserted into an orifice, exerting an outward force against an orifice wall, and retaining the orifice in an expanded state until removed. The expansor takes the form of a radial compression spring, adapted for transitioning between a compressed state, a partially expanded state, and an equilibrium state.
